The design articulates two interior areas through continuous millwork, vertical storage, and integrated work surfaces. In the kitchen, wood is combined with light veined stone and backlit modules to create a technical plane for preparation, storage, and display. In the closet, dark glass, black frames, and linear lighting organize clothing and accessories through controlled depth and transparency.

The kitchen wall is built in a walnut-tone wood with open shelving framed in black, interrupted by panels of translucent veined stone lit from behind. The same stone forms the backsplash and the fluted end of the island, whose wood front carries a strip of light below the counter and three bar stools. Built-in ovens, a tall column with an integrated refrigerator, and a dining table complete the room.

The closet is a walk-in room lined with dark glass doors on black frames, lit by continuous lines along the shelves. At its center, a block of the same veined stone works as an island, with a wood vanity and a mirror on one side.

Borsoga developed the interior design, 3D modeling, and visualization to represent the relationship between millwork, stone, glass, and artificial light emission.
