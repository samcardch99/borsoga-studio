The visualization explores the kitchen through a series of perspectives that reveal the relationship between material selection, surface finish, and spatial proportion. Warm wood tones are combined with stone and metal elements to define a composition that is both functional and visually precise.

A U-shaped perimeter wraps around a central island. Dark wood-grain upper cabinets and tall panels frame glass display cabinets lit from within, while the lower run is finished in a light grey matte surface. Grey-green veined stone covers the backsplash, the hood, and the island, which ends in an angled waterfall with a warm wood base and a band of light below the counter.

Four upholstered stools line the island under three translucent disc pendants. Overhead, a ceiling tray with cove lighting and recessed downlights balances the daylight from the window, and the black marble floor with white veining reflects both.

Borsoga developed the 3D modeling and visualization to represent the project's material character and spatial integration.
