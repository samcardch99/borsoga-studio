The design organizes the kitchen around two parallel islands, each clad in beige stone with warm golden veining and finished with waterfall ends. Both islands carry seating along one side, with wood bar stools upholstered in light fabric, so the room works for cooking and for gathering at the same time.

Behind them, a continuous wall of light wood millwork runs the full height of the space. It integrates tall storage, built-in ovens, and illuminated display niches with glass shelving, while the same stone returns on the hood and the backsplash to tie the work wall to the islands.

Above each island, a suspended stone panel with perimeter backlighting defines the zone and frames a linear pendant with glass globes. Recessed downlights complete the ceiling, and tall glass doors bring daylight across the light wood floor.

Borsoga developed the interior design, 3D modeling, and visualization to represent the relationship between stone, warm wood, integrated lighting, and the scale of a double-island layout.
