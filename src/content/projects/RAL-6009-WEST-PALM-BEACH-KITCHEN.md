The remodel opens the kitchen toward the dining and living areas and organizes it around a large rounded island that holds the sink and the seating. The perimeter is resolved as continuous wood millwork: arched frames contain the cooking wall and integrated ovens, while full-height panels conceal the refrigeration and storage. Green quartzite with gold veining covers the counters, the curved hood, the backsplash, and the coffee station, where floating stone shelves carry concealed LED lighting. A light travertine-toned floor keeps the room open around the wood and stone. Borsoga developed the interior design, millwork, material direction, 3D modeling, and visualization.
