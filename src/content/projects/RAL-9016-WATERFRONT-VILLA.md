The visualization represents a waterfront villa organized through horizontal planes, open terraces, and direct connection to the pool, dock, and exterior areas. White volumes contrast with the wood deck, black spiral stair, and tropical vegetation surrounding the property.

Seen from the water, the house sits behind a stone seawall and a wood dock where a boat is moored. Its upper level is lined with wood slats and climbing plants that spill over the roof edge, and wide glass walls open both floors to the view.

At the back, a pool finished in green and blue mosaic tile runs along a wood deck with loungers. A separate pavilion with a flat white roof and a wood ceiling holds an outdoor kitchen with a grill, open shelves, and a dining table, and the black spiral stair rises beside it toward the upper terraces.

Natural light defines the reading of the project across water, glass, light surfaces, and shaded exterior planes. Borsoga developed the 3D modeling and visualization to represent the project's scale, waterfront context, and relationship between architecture, landscape, and outdoor living areas.
