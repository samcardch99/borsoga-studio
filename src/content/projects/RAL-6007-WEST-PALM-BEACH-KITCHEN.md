The remodel joins rooms that were previously closed off and places the kitchen in the open space left by the demolition, with the sink on a large island as the client requested. The back wall is full-height wood millwork divided by rounded pilasters and arched panels. Its central arch frames a niche lined entirely in green stone, from the backsplash and lit shelves to the curved hood. Flush tall panels hold the refrigeration and a column of Miele ovens, and a second stone-lined niche houses the coffee station. The racetrack island repeats the arch on its seating side, where a recessed bay with concealed LED holds three counter stools, and its top continues the same stone with a thick rounded edge. A light travertine-toned floor, recessed downlights, and a linear pendant balance the darker wood and stone. Borsoga developed the interior design, millwork, material selection, 3D modeling, and visualization.
