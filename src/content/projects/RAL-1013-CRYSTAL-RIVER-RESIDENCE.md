The project documents a three-story residence through a combination of photography and CGI, capturing its material quality and atmosphere at different moments of the day. The work focuses on the relationship between natural light, surface texture, and the scale of the house within its tropical setting.

The house rises in stacked levels with deep covered balconies, black metal railings, and timber posts that carry the upper floors and a pitched roof with an exposed wood ceiling. An exterior stair climbs along the side façade, and a spiral stair connects the terraces at the back.

The views move from the driveway, with a stone path crossing the lawn, to the rear façade, where lounge chairs line a pool beneath the balconies. Palms and dense planting frame the house on every side.

Each image studies a different light: a blue sky with high clouds, a pink sunset that warms the façade, and dusk, when the downlights under each balcony and the garden lights take over. Borsoga developed the documentation and visualization to represent the project's final material state and spatial presence.
