The visualization presents a modern two-story residence through a series of exterior perspectives that reveal the relationship between volume, materiality, and light. The house is organized as stacked horizontal volumes in a soft gray finish, with an upper floor that cantilevers over the carport and the ground-floor terrace.

Vertical wood slats clad large parts of both levels and contrast with the mineral surfaces of the main volumes. Frameless glass railings run along the upper balcony, and floor-to-ceiling glazing with sheer curtains opens the interior toward the garden.

Thin lines of warm LED light trace the edges of the roof slabs and the carport frame, and recessed downlights mark the entrance. Low planting, gravel beds, and bollard lights organize the front yard between the driveway and the street.

The views move from the street, under a clear and then an overcast sky, to the side lawn framed by the canopy of mature trees, and close at dusk, when the artificial lighting draws the silhouette of the house. Borsoga developed the 3D modeling and visualization to represent the scale of the residence and the quality of its exterior environment.
