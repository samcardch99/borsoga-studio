The design organizes the kitchen and dining area through a longitudinal composition where the island, work wall, vertical storage, and dining table are connected along the same axis. Brown-grey millwork structures the main planes, while grey veined stone is applied to the island, backsplash, and hood to create mineral continuity in the preparation area.

The island has angled sides and seats three on upholstered stools in burnt orange, with a line of light under the counter. Above it, a recessed ceiling panel glows with warm light and holds three slim glass pendants. A tall glass display cabinet and a counter section in lighter wood continue the run toward the dining table, where the same orange seating returns around a long dark table.

Illuminated display cabinets, pendant lighting, and natural light from the large windows create contrast between warm artificial light and exterior daylight. Full-height sliding doors with sheer curtains open the room to the garden, and a light stone-look floor keeps the space bright against the dark millwork.

Borsoga developed the interior design, 3D modeling, and visualization to represent the relationship between kitchen, dining area, storage, and perimeter openness.
