Line 02

Social Media Design

Content designed as a visual system, not as isolated posts. Each format counts as a different product because the amount of design, narrative, adaptation and editing changes. The brand identity stays as the common base across every format.

Product 01

Static Post

A single graphic piece for one post. Composition, typography, image and adaptation to the brand identity.

Product 02

Carousel

Multi-slide content organised as a coherent visual narrative, with hierarchy and continuity from slide to slide.

Product 03

Story

A vertical piece for Stories. It can stand alone or adapt a campaign or a main post.

Product 04

Reel / Short-Form Video

Vertical video editing and design: pacing, text, graphics, transitions and visual assets.

Product 05

Motion Post

An animated graphic or motion graphic, with no need to start from video footage.

Product 06

Social Media Templates

A reusable template system so the brand stays visually consistent in future posts.

How scope is set

A static post, a carousel and a reel don't count as the same unit of work. Each format carries a different level of design, structure, editing and production; that's why each can be quoted on its own or combined into monthly plans.
