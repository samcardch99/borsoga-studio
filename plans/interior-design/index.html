What applies to all three plans

Where we work

Essential and Premium in Miami-Dade. Borsoga Edition across Florida, with no travel charge.

How many spaces

From a single room to the whole house. There's no minimum: you can hire us for one space.

How many images

It depends on the size of each space, not on the plan: 3 images for a small space, 5 for a medium one, 8 for a large one.

The files

Everything arrives in formats you can open and share: images as JPG or PNG, drawings and documents as PDF, video as MP4.
